Communication job
By the end, enterprise decision-makers should understand that Auto Reports keeps JIRA as the system of record while reducing the navigation and recombination needed to answer recurring operational questions.

Source structure
- 10-slide 16:9 deck, 1280 x 720.
- White canvas, Noto Sans KR typography, dark navy text, blue accent, thin gray rules.
- Source slides 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, and 10 provide the required opening, comparison, screenshot, workflow, architecture, roadmap, and decision frames.

Reusable slide types
- Slide 1: minimal cover with blue vertical accent.
- Slides 3 and 6: text plus large product screenshot.
- Slide 4: five-step vertical workflow.
- Slide 5: large screenshot plus three evidence bullets.
- Slide 2: three-row comparison plus thesis panel.
- Slide 8: four-node operating model.
- Slide 9: three-phase implementation roadmap.
- Slide 10: three-part decision frame.

Inherited placeholders and furniture
- The source deck uses slide-local text and shapes rather than empty structural placeholders.
- Page labels, title rules, screenshot frames, bullets, arrows, and architecture nodes remain inherited and are edited only through mapped element IDs.

Typography and spacing
- Preserve source font family, font size, weight, line spacing, alignment, and text-box geometry.
- Shorten copy rather than shrinking text.

Insertion contract
- Every output slide duplicates one source slide.
- No new narrative primitives are added.
- Screenshot swaps preserve inherited image placement and crop behavior.
- The roadmap uses the inherited three-phase timeline without changing its geometry.
- Speaker notes are rewritten with claim-specific [Sources] blocks.

Source inspection
- Every source slide render, layout JSON, extracted media set, and the full inspect snapshot was reviewed.
